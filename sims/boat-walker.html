Walking on a boat, with and without drag

A person of mass mp walks from one end of a boat of mass mb and length L to the other. On frictionless water the boat slides back a distance mpL / (mp + mb) and stays there. Add a linear drag force F = −k vb and the boat drifts back to where it started, while the center of mass of the system moves.

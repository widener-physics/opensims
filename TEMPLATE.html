Projectile on a slope

A ball is launched from the foot of a slope. The dashed line is where it will go; the solid trail is where it has been. Change the launch angle and watch where it lands on the slope. This file is the template: replace the model, the drawing, and this text.

0.00 s
Time of flight
T = 2v0 sin(θ − α) / (g cos α)
Distance along the slope
R = v0T cos(θ − α) − ½g sin α T2
Maximum height above the slope
H = v02 sin2(θ − α) / (2g cos α)

What to try. Find the launch angle that carries the ball furthest up the slope; it is not 45°. Set the slope to zero and check that the range matches the flat-ground formula.

Units: metres and seconds, g = 9.8 m/s², no air resistance. The ball is a point; the slope is a straight line through the launch point.
